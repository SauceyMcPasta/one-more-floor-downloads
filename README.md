# One More Floor: Tiny Criminal

Standalone third-person raccoon stealth heist for Windows 10/11 x64.

## Download and play

[Download OneMoreFloorLauncher.exe](https://github.com/SauceyMcPasta/one-more-floor-downloads/releases/latest/download/OneMoreFloorLauncher.exe)

**Launcher 1.1.0 lets you choose your game drive.** Close the old launcher, then download its replacement above. Click **CHOOSE FOLDER**, select a location such as `M:\Games\OneMoreFloor`, and click **INSTALL** or **INSTALL HERE**. Both the temporary download and game files go there. The launcher remembers the location and checks free space before installing. Game saves stay in their existing location. Game updates do not replace the launcher itself.

Run the launcher, click Install, then Play. Existing players: click Update for **v0.11**. Repair installs a fresh verified copy; installed games can be played offline.

## Current gameplay

- Explore to reveal the minimap. Entering a room reveals it for the current floor.
- Varied rooms and two small closets connect to shared hallways. Players and guards open and close doors.
- Search furniture for valuables and safe clues; search a knocked-out guard for the guaranteed security keycard.
- Unlock the secure room, then crack its safe with one of five randomized minigames: PIN, symbols, invoice total, circuit switches or a combination dial. Required clues always exist outside the locked room.
- Tab / Y opens the clue notebook. Guards keep moving during notebook reading and safe cracking.
- Disarm a guard's flashlight, then attack once more to knock them out for seven seconds. Otherwise they kneel and search for the flashlight for five seconds before resuming rounds.
- Guards patrol rooms twice, take a ten-second break, then change their rounds route. Their bodies and flashlights turn together while looking around.
- Lockers and trash cans appear in selected randomized locations and allow hiding from any accessible side. Hide under desks from their open side.
- The building stays dark during play and alerts. Squeak, combo fighting, the breaker and floating room labels are removed.
- Device-aware button prompts show interactions and potential search rewards. Small item symbols sit below the bag icon.
- Body or safe-theft discovery summons three support guards through the elevator. Hide unseen and unheard for five seconds before threat can fall; clear it completely to send support away.
- The elevator requires zero threat and opens the upgrade shop after each floor. Capture resets floor, haul, stash, records and upgrades.
- Regular rooms: floors 1–5 have 9, floors 6–10 have 11, floors 11+ have 13, plus one guard room and two closets.
- Regular guards: floors 1–3 have 2, floors 4–8 have 3, floors 9+ have 4.

## Controls

WASD moves; mouse orbits; Space jumps. Left click disarms/attacks; right click searches, opens doors, hides or interacts. Shift sprints, Ctrl crouches, Z toggles the flashlight, Tab opens clues. Controller: X/Square attacks, B/Circle interacts, A/Cross jumps/confirms, Y/Triangle opens clues, RB/R1 flashlight, RT/R2 sprint, LT/L2 crouch. Escape / Start pauses.

## About this build

Early prototype with procedural art. Windows executables are unsigned. Automated game, export and installation checks pass; Windows visuals, physical controllers, audio and difficulty still need hands-on playtesting. Radio speech uses an available English system voice; captions and sound effects work without one.

Game saves: `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\One More Floor`. Launcher state: `%APPDATA%\OneMoreFloorLauncher`.

This repository contains player downloads and patch notes. Private source is kept separately. No account or subscription is needed to play.
