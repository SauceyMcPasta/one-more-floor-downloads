# One More Floor: Tiny Criminal

Standalone third-person raccoon stealth heist for Windows 10/11 x64.

## Download and play

[Download OneMoreFloorLauncher.exe](https://github.com/SauceyMcPasta/one-more-floor-downloads/releases/latest/download/OneMoreFloorLauncher.exe)

Run the launcher, click Install, then Play. It checks for new releases; click Update when available. Repair installs a fresh verified copy, and installed games can be played offline.

## Current gameplay

- The building stays dark during play and alerts. Only capture turns room lights on.
- Cut the wall breaker to release secured goods. Its single lever has a one-second toggle cooldown.
- Rare valuables pay more; upgrade base prices are Pockets $700, Sneakers $900 and Endurance $800.
- Guards follow regular patrol loops and periodically visit the additional guard break room.
- Regular rooms: floors 1–5 have 9, floors 6–10 have 11, floors 11+ have 13, plus one guard room.
- Regular guards: floors 1–3 have 2, floors 4–8 have 3, floors 9+ have 4.
- Interact to hide in lockers, trash cans or under desks. Guards who see you enter or hear noise from inside can check that spot.
- Guards recover after 20 seconds. Discovering downed coworkers or missing rare valuables summons three support guards through the elevator, seals the goods and starts radio/alarm calls.
- Remain unseen and unheard for five seconds before alert decay starts, then stay hidden until the bar empties. Support guards return through the elevator after clearance.
- Small item symbols sit directly below the bag icon, without names or cash values.
- The elevator requires an empty threat bar. Every completed floor opens the upgrade shop; purchases use haul first, then stash.
- Capture resets floor, haul, stash, records and all upgrades. Sound preferences stay unchanged.

## Controls

WASD moves, mouse orbits, Space jumps. Left click attacks; right click collects/interacts/hides/leaves. Shift sprints, Ctrl crouches, E squeaks, Z toggles the flashlight. Controller: X/Square attacks, B/Circle interacts, A/Cross jumps, Y/Triangle squeaks, RB/R1 flashlight, RT/R2 sprint, LT/L2 crouch.

## About this build

Early prototype with procedural art. Windows executables are unsigned. Radio speech uses an available English system voice; captions and sound effects work without one. Windows visuals, physical controls and economy tuning still need playtesting.

Game saves: `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\One More Floor`. Launcher state and installations: `%APPDATA%\OneMoreFloorLauncher`.

This repository contains player downloads and patch notes. Game source is kept separately. No account or subscription is needed to download and play.
